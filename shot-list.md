# Mother Tongues: archival shot list

Every photo slot in the stories accepts a drag-and-drop preview. Send the final files back and they'll be embedded permanently. Prefer public-domain or openly licensed images, and record the credit line for each.

## Hawaiian story
| Slot | What to find | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Honolulu harbour or Hilo Bay, c. 1890, with ships | Hawaiʻi State Archives digital collections; Wikimedia Commons "Honolulu Harbor 1890s" |
| 1893 · The overthrow | Queen Liliʻuokalani portrait, c. 1891 (face near top centre: the annotation circles it) | Wikimedia Commons "Liliuokalani" (public domain) |
| 1983 · Fewer than fifty children | An early Pūnana Leo class, 1980s | Ask ʻAha Pūnana Leo for permission; or a community member's family photo |
| Intro portrait | A kupuna (elder) who spoke Hawaiian, photographed by family | Community contribution, with permission |
| Optional | Front page of *Ka Nupepa Kuokoa* | Papakilo Database; Chronicling America (Library of Congress) |

## Audio
- Hawaiian: a short recording of a native speaker (Ka Leo Hawaiʻi radio archive, University of Hawaiʻi). Ask for permission.
- Irish: a Conamara or Blasket speaker (RTÉ Archives, or the Doegen Records at the Royal Irish Academy, 1928–31).
